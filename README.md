## 🔭 What Is AetherLens?

AetherLens is not merely a fork; it is a re-imagining. Where the ancestral alchemy-tool was a sturdy workshop bench, AetherLens is a pair of spectacles — one that reads what is already on your screen and turns it into a quiet stream of usable knowledge. You do not type numbers into a form. You do not transcribe a panel of icons. You **capture**, and the lens handles the rest.

It is a screenshot OCR autofill engine fused with a craft copilot, tuned specifically for the Qi Multi optimizer ecosystem inside *Chasing Immortality*. Whether you are testing a build mockup, cross-checking a friend's screenshot, or verifying a strategy before committing resources, AetherLens collapses the distance between what you see and what you can act on.

The project ships under the MIT license (a heritage nod to the WTFPL roots of its ancestry, but with a tidier legal envelope suited to 2026).

---

## 🌱 Why This Exists

There is a peculiar friction in incremental-cultivation games: the numbers matter enormously, yet the interface rarely gives you a clean export. You stare at a panel. You tab between browser windows. You jot numbers into a spreadsheet. You make a typo. You re-check. You sigh.

AetherLens was born from that sigh.

Instead of demanding that the player become a data-entry clerk, the lens offers an alternative ritual: capture the evidence, and let a scripted intelligence propose the craft. It is the difference between measuring a river with a ruler and simply dipping a cup.

---

## 🧘 Core Philosophy

1. **Capture Over Transcription.** Your eyes are for strategy, not for copying digits.
2. **Propose, Don't Command.** The copilot suggests; you decide. Autonomy stays with the player.
3. **Local First, Cloud Optional.** Screenshots belong to the person who took them.
4. **Many Tongues, One Lens.** Language should never be a gate to insight.
5. **Quiet Interfaces Win.** A good tool gets out of the way.

## 🔬 How the Lens Sees

The pipeline is short, and deliberately so:

1. **Ingest** — a screenshot arrives via drag, paste, file picker, or capture overlay.
2. **Normalize** — the image is scaled, contrast-adjusted, and deskewed so the OCR stage faces a friendly surface.
3. **Segment** — regions of interest are isolated: icon grids, numeric panels, resource bars.
4. **Recognize** — characters are extracted per region, with per-field confidence.
5. **Reconcile** — recognized values are validated against known field ranges; implausible reads are flagged, not silently trusted.
6. **Populate** — the optimizer's input schema is filled in, and a diff is shown so you can approve or amend.

Each stage is swappable. If you dislike the default normalizer, replace it. The lens does not argue.

## 🧭 The Copilot Layer

The copilot is not an oracle. It is a well-read companion who has memorized the shape of many player journeys. It reasons in three currencies:

- **Resources** — what you have, what you will have, and what you will wish you had.
- **Tempo** — how long a path takes, and whether patience or haste serves you better.
- **Regret** — the invisible cost of a craft you cannot undo.

Every suggestion arrives with a small ledger: the resources it assumes, the tempo it predicts, and the regret it believes you are avoiding. You may disagree with all three, and the copilot will simply update its model.

## 🌍 Multilingual Cultivation

Language is not decoration; it is infrastructure. AetherLens treats translation as a first-class citizen:

- Locale files are plain, diff-friendly, and versioned alongside code.
- Pluralization rules follow CLDR patterns, not naive string concatenation.
- Dates render in the format a local reader expects, not the format a server happens to emit.
- The copilot's rationale strings are translatable, so the *why* never lapses into a foreign tongue.

If you speak a language the lens does not yet carry, the contribution path is short and appreciated.

## 📐 Responsive by Design

Responsiveness here means more than shrinking. The interface is a set of panels that renegotiate their arrangement depending on available real estate:

- **Narrow** — a vertical cascade: capture, preview, results.
- **Medium** — two columns: image on the left, findings on the right.
- **Wide** — a triptych: source, recognition, and copilot, all visible at once.
- **Ultra-wide** — a fourth column for scenario history, so context never scrolls away.

Nothing hides. Nothing gets amputated. The layout merely changes its posture.

## 🛠️ Designing Your Workflow

AetherLens does not prescribe a single path. Common rhythms include:

- **The Verifier** — capture a friend's screenshot, confirm their claimed numbers, and validate a shared strategy.
- **The Archivist** — save scenarios weekly and watch your progress compound as a diff timeline.
- **The Experimenter** — fork a scenario, mutate one variable, and observe how the copilot's advice shifts.
- **The Teacher** — export a Markdown summary of a scenario to accompany a guide or a forum post.

Each rhythm uses the same primitives: capture, recognize, reconcile, decide.

---

## 🗂️ Scenario Library

Scenarios are the lens's memory. Each scenario is a portable document containing:

- The captured image reference (or a hash, if you prefer not to embed the image).
- The recognized field map with confidence scores.
- The optimizer inputs, post-reconciliation.
- Optional annotations — a note, a tag, a color.
- A version stamp so future tools can migrate old scenarios forward.

Because scenarios are plain files, they travel well. Email them, commit them, paste them into a chat. No proprietary container, no lock-in.

## 🔐 Data Handling & Privacy

- **Default: local.** OCR and copilot reasoning happen on your machine.
- **Opt-in: sync.** If you enable cross-device sync, you choose the transport.
- **Never: telemetry-by-surprise.** No silent pings, no hidden beacons.
- **Always: your screenshots.** The lens reads them; it does not claim them.

If a feature cannot be built without compromising these tenets, the feature waits.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this affiliated with the original alchemy-tool?**
It is a spiritual descendant, rebuilt from the ground up. The ancestral project's spirit of generosity is honored; the code and interface are new.

**Do I have to let the copilot decide for me?**
Never. The copilot proposes. You remain the cultivator.

**Can I use this without an internet connection?**
Yes. The default configuration is entirely offline.

**Will my screenshots end up somewhere unexpected?**
Not unless you explicitly enable a sync feature and choose a destination.

**What happens if the OCR misreads a digit?**
The reconciliation stage flags low-confidence fields and asks you to confirm before the value is committed.

**Is there a way to contribute a translation?**
Yes. Locale files are plain text and the contribution path is documented in the project's contributor notes.
